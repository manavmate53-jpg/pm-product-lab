PRD-KYC-2026.04 · VeriShield Engine v2.4 · Author: Manav Mate (Product Manager)

B2B Compliance & Document Risk Engine (PRD)

Technical Product Requirements Document defining automated onboarding verification, fuzzy entity reconciliation, API specifications, and straight-through processing targets for Indian enterprise merchants.

1. Problem Statement

38% Merchant Onboarding Drop-off from Rigid KYC Checks

Staffing agencies and B2B platforms in India suffer high drop-offs during merchant onboarding because legacy KYC software relies on exact string matches. A legitimate business with name variations (for example: "Pvt Ltd" vs "Private Limited", or "Services" vs "Ops LLP") gets hard-rejected.

• Turnaround Delay: Manual review takes 4 to 24 hours per applicant.
• Revenue Impact: High-intent merchants churn to competitors during waiting periods.
• False Positive Rate: 14.2% of rejected merchants were fully compliant.
2. Solution Architecture

Hybrid OCR + Levenshtein Matching + Confidence Thresholds

VeriShield implements a 4-tier pipeline combining format checksum regex, cross-field PAN-GSTIN validation, and normalized Levenshtein string distance. Applications are classified into 3 deterministic routing buckets:

• Score > 85: Auto-Approve (Straight-Through Processing)
• Score 60 to 85: Route to 1-Click Ops Review Queue
• Score < 60: Auto-Reject & Trigger Compliance Webhook

PRD-MKT-2026.08 · AutoMatch IQ Engine v3.1 · Author: Manav Mate (Product Manager)

Marketplace Discovery & Funnel Telemetry Engine (PRD)

Product Requirements Document detailing consumer choice overload mitigation, event stream taxonomy, zero-result recommendation fallback, and seller distribution mechanics.

1. Problem & Core Hypothesis

88.8% Drop-Off Driven by "Paradox of Choice"

Traditional used car portals present users with 18+ unranked filters. First-time buyers experience decision paralysis, spend an average of 4.2 minutes fiddling with checkboxes, and abandon without scheduling an inspection.

Core Hypothesis: Guiding buyers through a 3-step intent questionnaire (Commute → Monthly TCO → Must-Have Priority) limits cognitive load, surfaces the Top 3 relevant choices, and doubles search-to-lead conversion.
2. Event Tracking Taxonomy

Granular Product Telemetry Specs

quiz_step_completed

Triggered on step selection. Props: step_key, value, duration_ms.

tco_compare_drawer_opened

Triggered when 2 cars are selected. Props: car_a_id, car_b_id, tco_diff.

high_intent_lead_submitted

Key North Star event. Props: listing_id, cohort, buyer_intent.

3. Zero-Result Fallback Logic

Guaranteed Inventory Discovery

When restrictive filter combinations yield 0 matching listings, the recommendation engine executes a 3-tier cascade rather than showing a dead-end screen:

1. Geo Radius Expansion: Automatically expands radius by +15 km (e.g. Powai → Thane).
2. Price Elasticity: Relaxes budget boundary by ±10% with high-TCO offsets.
3. Collaborative Fit: Surfaces vehicles selected by buyers with identical commute profiles.
4. Seller Routing & Response SLAs

Fair Dealer Allocation & Response Guardrails

High-intent inspection leads are dispatched according to dealer tier and response velocity to maximize conversion:

• 15-Minute Response Cap: Dealer must confirm appointment in 15 mins or lead reroutes.
• Lead Rotation: Round-robin distribution among top-rated sellers (inspection grade ≥ 9.0).
• Fraud Prevention: Phone verification and device fingerprinting to stop phantom leads.
